## Game hooks found in the files
- Incident vs. Problem maps onto glitches vs. the simulation: small glitches repeat until players realize the system itself is the problem. Signal's rule (5+ incidents in 90 days = Problem) can become a clue mechanic.
- Know Your Lens self-inventory → character creation.
- Four meters matching the program tracks: Tech, Business, Leadership, Wellness.
- Mentor feedback moments → "System Review" trials (Q&A gauntlet, no-tablet challenge).
- Resolution Plan → mid-game communication crisis.
- SOP challenge → early warm-up mini-game.

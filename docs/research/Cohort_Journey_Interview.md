# Cohort Journey Interview (draft, Nov 8, 2026)

**Status:** Myia is reviewing the questions. Next step: build the Google Form from her edits, then schedule it to send Monday.

Purpose: collect each cycle mate's own point of view on the Cycle 60 journey so the game reflects their story, not just their profile data. The personality packet and IDR show *how* people work; this interview captures *what the journey felt like to them*.

Who: the 16 active cycle mates. Do not interview or reference departed interns; they stay as code-named ghosts only.

How: a ~5-minute Google Form (Myia's choice) so answers land in a sheet the build team can use. Offer an anonymous option.

## Questions (form type · why · where it goes in the game)
1. **In one word, how did you feel on your first day? Why?** Short answer. Easy, low-pressure opener. → Elevator arrival lines, Boot Up round.
2. **What's one moment from the cycle you'll never forget? What happened, and roughly when?** Paragraph. The heart of the form: real moments; "roughly when" places it in the right round. → Trial scenario cards.
3. **When did you feel most stuck, or closest to "glitching"? What got you through it?** Paragraph. Uses the game's own word; the recovery half matters most. → Glitch and reboot messages, Sprint 3 (Grit) cards.
4. **Who or what helped you most? (A teammate, a ritual like All Skate or High Tea, a workshop, a mentor)** Paragraph. Gifts stand for real support. → Gift and Lifeline moments.
5. **Which phase was hardest for you, and why?** Multiple choice (Boot Up, Sprint 1, The Mirror, Geek Week, Sprint 2, Sprint 3) + short why. The one question that gives numbers. → Round costs and balance.
6. **Is there an inside joke, phrase or moment only Cycle 60 would get?** Short answer, optional. → Easter eggs and ghost whispers.
7. **What's one thing you can do now that you couldn't do in week one?** Short answer. The growth story. → Sprint 3 cards, Graduation screen.
8. **If this game could teach the next cycle one thing, what should it be?** Short answer. → Ghost hints, final debrief message.
9. **Is there anything about your journey you do NOT want in the game?** Paragraph, optional. Protects people. → Opt-out list.
10. **Can we use your answers with your name, or only anonymously?** Multiple choice (With my name / Anonymously / Don't use my answers). → Consent flag.

Add the full round flow, sabotage, accusations, and win conditions to the existing Performance_Review: Exit_ProtocoL app. Keep the characters and economy already built.

ROUND FLOW (rounds 1 to 6)

The host clicks Next to move through each step. Show the current step on every screen.

a. System Briefing: reveal the round's trial name and attribute.
b. Open Floor: players chat, switch teams (max 4 per team), send gifts. Contrarians may sabotage.
c. The Trial: teams play offline. The host enters Win or Loss per team. Every player pays the trial cost.
d. Glitch Report: show results. Each team may submit one accusation. Then open a 30-second gift window with a countdown. When it closes, lock in glitches.
e. Stats Update: players place earned points. Backup refills by 1.

Round 7 is Graduation.

SABOTAGE (Contrarians only, optional)

- During Open Floor. Costs 1 backup.
- Drains 2 points from the target's current trial attribute. If the target's Composure is 6 or more, drain 1.
- Limit 1 sabotage per Contrarian per round. (assumed)
- The target does not see who did it.

ACCUSATIONS

- At Glitch Report, each team may submit one accusation against a player not on their team.
- Correct (the accused sabotaged this round): every member of the accusing team gets +1 point (their choice). The caught Contrarian loses 2 points total, no matter how many teams were right. A caught Contrarian can never sabotage again and loses the Exploit path, but can still graduate.
- Wrong: the player who submitted it loses 1 attribute point (their choice).

WIN CONDITIONS (both sides can win in the same game)

- Graduate (everyone): at round 7, total attribute points 22 or more, every attribute 2 or more, not Stuck. Backup does not count. (assumed) Round 7 points are placed before the check. (assumed)
- Lifeline (Cooperative): cancel 2 glitches for teammates with gift-window gifts. The same teammate can count twice.
- Auditor (Independent): be on a team that makes 2 correct accusations.
- Exploit (Contrarian): complete 3 sabotages that are not caught that round.
- A second-path win finished before getting Stuck is kept.

Track progress on each second path for the host. Show each player only their own progress.

SCREENS

- Round screen: round number, step, trial name and attribute, timer during the gift window, my numbers, everyone's status flags.
- Gift, sabotage, and accusation actions as buttons that only appear in the right step and for the right players.
- Host control panel: Next step button, Win or Loss per team, full view of all players.
- Results screen: players who graduated see a "real world" screen where the title is fixed to "Performance_Review: Exit_Protocol". Players who did not see "Your contract has been renewed." Show any second-path wins.
- Debrief screen for the host: graduation count, glitches, second-path wins, sabotages caught.

Do not build the clue system or special abilities. Those are not designed yet.

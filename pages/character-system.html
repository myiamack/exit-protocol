The Pixel Intern, and who's around them

Every player is a Pixel Intern. The other concepts get jobs of their own: Screen-Heads and Meeples teach, office objects become tools, the red cursor drives the menus, and holograms show when someone is glitched.

1

Build your Pixel Intern

Try it

Start with Masculine (the original intern) or Feminine (defined waist and hips, softer chin). Then mix any hair, skin tone, outfit and accessory. Nothing is locked to one or the other. Tap anyone in the lineup to load their look.

2

Instructors

Press Next

Instructors appear in How to Play and at the start of each level. Screen-Heads explain the system: glitches and the Contrarian. When they finish talking about glitches, they glitch. Meeples teach the Cooperative side, the players who win by helping each other. A Hologram teaches the Independents.

3

Tools: office objects, in pixels

Proposed · team to approve

Same idea as before, redrawn as pixel items so they match the interns. Pick one tool before Level 1. Each ability uses rules you already have.

4

The cursor

Move your mouse here, then click a button

Decided: menus only. On the start page, menu and wardrobe, the pointer is the red arrow. The hand, spinner and target below stay as ideas only, so gameplay screens keep the normal pointer. Every click makes it glitch for a split second, then it snaps back to red. On phones there's no pointer, so tapped buttons flash the same glitch instead.

5

On the office map

Interns walk the map as their pixel selves. Holograms have two jobs here:

Role A · Glitched players

When a real player is Glitching or Stuck, they show as a cyan hologram of their own intern. Graduates turn solid again on the exit screen.

Role B · Wandering ghosts

Three default interns who glitched out of the program. They're not players. They drift through the office, fade in and out at random, and sometimes leave behind an anomaly clue.

● Solid = OK● Cyan hologram = player Glitching or Stuck● Pale flickering ghost = wandering ghost (watch them move)● Small item by the feet = equipped tool

Exit_ProtocoL · Character direction

Pick who walks the office

Six original directions. Each card shows three color variants and how that character looks when it glitches. Pick one, or mix two (for example, a Meeple body with a Screen-Head face).

How to choose: think about what the room should feel at the facilitation. Cute and social? Sharp and techy? Board-game first? The character sets that tone more than any other piece.
